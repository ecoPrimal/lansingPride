# Lansing Pride Incident — Saturday, June 28, 2026

**Complainant**: Kevin Mok (US Army infantry veteran)
**Location**: Entrance to the Brenke Fish Ladder, Lansing, MI
(within the Lansing Pride Festival perimeter)
**Date/Time**: Saturday, June 28, 2026, approximately 1:00 PM
**Status**: Charges pressed. Police report filed. Card received from officer.

## Narrative (written same day, immediately after returning home)

### Before the incident

Kevin and Heather attended Lansing Pride. Parked right at the
entrance on Cesar E. Chavez (westbound, directly at start of
barriers). Went into Odd Nodd Art Supply for art supplies — Kevin
purchased modeling clay. Traveled between various booths down
Cesar E. Chavez. Heather exchanged business cards at several
booths, including with a lawyer and a Lansing housing advocate/
civil servant. She purchased a spine in a jar from an art shop
and items from other booths.

Heather stopped at a booth next to a VOA (Volunteers of America)
pop-up, then went into the VOA tent (the event sells risque
donated items). She was taking her time, so Kevin walked down to
the river bank.

### The altercation

Kevin was at the river bank near the entrance to the Brenke Fish
Ladder (~1:00 PM). The sound of food truck engines had him thinking
about how the fish ladder could be running them instead with no
noise. Behind him and to the left, he heard
something like "fuck off fat ass" and looked over his shoulder.

**The victims**: 3-4 young girls. Youngest appeared approximately
12-13 years old. Oldest could not have been 18. The oldest had
brown and blonde hair, split horizontally about halfway up.

Kevin initially thought it might be a loud family interaction.
He turned back to the water. The yelling continued. The oldest
girl was telling the male (albeit vulgarly) to leave them alone.

**The aggressor**: Male. Taller than Kevin by a few inches.
Approximately 100 lbs heavier than Kevin. Grey ball cap. Grey
facial hair. Wearing a light blue shirt with a monogram (not
fully caught). He was standing BEHIND the chair swing near the
girls.

### Kevin's intervention

Kevin realized this was an altercation, turned, and began walking
toward them. The male was continuing to say something to the girls.
The girls were telling him to leave them alone.

Kevin walked behind the chair swing and stood within 2-3 feet of
the male. Hands up at shoulder level. Said: **"Hey man, why don't we
leave the girls alone?"**

Male: "Shut the fuck up."

Kevin: **"We need to leave them alone."**

Male responded with vulgarities. At this point Kevin was paying
attention to the girls — the male was distracted with Kevin. The
male pivoted his body and moved toward Kevin. Kevin continued to
repeat that he needed to leave the girls alone. The male continued
to step toward Kevin. Kevin gave only inches as the male got in
his face.

**The girls fled north on the sidewalk past the vendors.**

### Physical assault

The male was now locked into Kevin. Stepping forward. He then
began to strike Kevin in the face:

- **Open palm, straight arm, right-handed — to face and nose only**
- **Two-handed shoves** — after each shove, continued stepping
  forward into the space created
- All strikes targeted the face/nose. No strikes to body.
- Attempted to physically push his body past Kevin
- Kevin yelled: **"STOP ASSAULTING ME"**
- Male struck again (face/nose, same method)

More attention was drawn. Girls were fully out of the area. They
were now near the drink tent. Kevin had been backing that direction
and placing himself between the male and the direction the girls
fled the entire time.

**Drink tent staff**: Said they called security and told both to
cut it out. When Kevin heard them, he put his hands up in the air,
said **"He assaulted me"**, and stepped back with hands raised.

### Second confrontation

The male then began to head down the stairs in the same direction
the girls had fled. Kevin followed from a distance of approximately
10 feet. The male stopped at the top of the stairs.

Kevin: "I'm leaving." Then: "OK, you can leave."

The male turned around, walked toward Kevin, and **body checked
him**, saying: "What if I want to leave this way?"

Kevin stood firm with hands raised in the air. The male **struck
Kevin in the face again**, then turned around and walked away into
the crowd of vendors.

Kevin followed at a further distance. The girls were well out of
sight.

### Police response

Police and security intervened. The male began telling his "story"
— claimed he was a kind citizen and Kevin stopped him from leaving
after he was chastising litterbugs.

Kevin told the officer: **"I'm a US veteran and I am pressing
charges."**

Officers separated and conducted interviews. Kevin could feel his
adrenaline pumping. He saw Heather in the distance leaving the VOA
tent and shouted to get her attention, filled her in.

Kevin found himself constantly returning to parade rest as energy
wound down. He spoke with both officers and the security team.

Kevin stated to officers:
- He is a US infantry veteran
- He is fully capable of defending himself
- **At no point did he strike the aggressor or attempt to defend
  himself physically during the altercation**
- He intervened solely to create space for the girls to escape

Kevin received a card and a debrief from the officer. Told them he
intended to leave to recoup while Heather continued at the festival.

### Return home

Kevin drove home. Parked in the road — his driveway was filled
with bikes (neighborhood kids at the house). Walked down to the
basement and typed this report.
