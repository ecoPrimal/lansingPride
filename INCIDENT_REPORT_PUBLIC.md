# Lansing Pride Incident — Saturday, June 27, 2026

**Author**: Kevin — US Army infantry veteran, data scientist
**Location**: Brenke Fish Ladder, Lansing, MI
(within the Lansing Pride Festival perimeter)
**Date/Time**: Saturday, June 27, 2026, approximately 1:00 PM
**Status**: Charges pressed. Police report filed.

---

## What happened

My partner and I attended Lansing Pride. We parked near the
entrance on Cesar E. Chavez, hit up Odd Nodd Art Supply, walked
the booths. She stopped at a VOA tent and was taking her time,
so I walked down to the river bank near the Brenke Fish Ladder.

I was standing by the water, listening to food truck engines and
thinking about how the fish ladder could be powering them
instead. Behind me and to the left I heard something like
"fuck off fat ass" and looked over my shoulder.

**3-4 young girls.** Youngest looked about 12-13. Oldest couldn't
have been 18 — brown and blonde hair, split horizontally about
halfway up. I thought it might be a loud family interaction. I
turned back to the water. The yelling continued. The oldest girl
was telling a man (albeit vulgarly) to leave them alone.

**The man**: Taller than me by a few inches. Had about 100 lbs
on me. Grey ball cap. Grey facial hair. Light blue shirt with
a monogram I didn't fully catch. He was standing BEHIND the
chair swing near the girls.

I realized this was an altercation. Turned and walked toward
them. He was still saying something to them. The girls were
telling him to leave them alone.

I walked behind the chair swing and stood within 2-3 feet of
him. Hands up at shoulder level. Said: **"Hey man, why don't we
leave the girls alone?"**

"Shut the fuck up."

**"We need to leave them alone."**

He responded with vulgarities. At this point I was paying
attention to the girls — he was distracted with me. He pivoted
and moved toward me. I continued to repeat he needed to leave
them alone. He continued to step toward me. I gave only inches
as he got in my face.

**The girls fled north on the sidewalk past the vendors.**

### The assault

He was locked into me now. Stepping forward. He began to strike
me in the face:

- **Open palm, straight arm, right-handed — to face and nose only**
- **Two-handed shoves** — after each shove, continued stepping
  forward into the space created
- All strikes targeted my face/nose. No strikes to body.
- Attempted to physically push past me
- I yelled: **"STOP ASSAULTING ME"**
- He struck again (face/nose, same method)

More attention was drawn. Girls were fully out of the area. We
were now near the drink tent. I had been backing that direction
and placing myself between him and the direction the girls fled
the entire time.

**Drink tent staff** said they called security and told both of
us to cut it out. When I heard them, I put my hands fully in
the air, said **"He assaulted me"**, and stepped back with hands
raised.

### He came back

He then began to head down the stairs in the same direction the
girls had fled. I followed from about 10 feet. He stopped at
the top of the stairs.

"I'm leaving." Then: "OK, you can leave."

He turned around, walked toward me, and **body checked me**,
saying: "What if I want to leave this way?"

I stood firm with hands raised in the air. He **struck me in the
face again**, then turned around and walked away into the crowd
of vendors.

The girls were fully out of sight and security had been alerted.
Imminent threat resolved. I took three photos of him from the
top of the stairs, then followed at 20+ feet and flagged police
officers when I saw them.

### Police

Police and security intervened. He began to tell his "story" —
claimed he was a kind citizen and I stopped him from leaving
after he was chastising litterbugs.

I told the officer: **"I'm a US veteran and I am pressing
charges."**

They separated us, did interviews. I could feel my blood
pumping. I saw my partner in the distance leaving a tent and
shouted to get her attention.

Adrenaline coursing through me, I found myself constantly
returning to parade rest. Talked to both officers and the
security team. I let them know I'm a US infantry veteran, fully
capable of defending myself, and **at no point did I strike him
or attempt to defend myself physically.** I intervened solely to
create space for the girls to escape.

Got a card, got a debrief. Told them I intended to leave to
recoup while my partner continued at the festival.

### After

Drove home. Parked in the road — my driveway was filled with
bikes (neighborhood kids at the house). Walked down to the
basement and typed this up.

A friend texted me on Messenger about running into my partner at
the festival. I finished breathing, then drove back to Pride for
a couple more hours while she continued to canvass booths.

The assault took place approximately 100 feet from a lawyer my
partner had been speaking with 15 minutes earlier.

## Morning after (Sunday, June 28)

- No visible bruising
- Nose slightly tender
- Neck and back muscles tight on right side (from resisting
  repeated blows to the face while maintaining position)

### What he was actually doing

The straight-arm palm thrust to the nose is a well-known and
taught combative technique designed to cause serious grievous
harm — broken nose, disorientation, potential cartilage damage.
This was not flailing. This was deliberate, targeted, and
repeated. He struck with intent to injure.

The reason I sustained minimal damage: I'm a trained infantry
combatant. I recognized the incoming strikes and absorbed the
impact. He was taller, which reduced the effectiveness of the
downward angle. The damage was minimized entirely by my training
and composure — not by his restraint.

### Who this person is

This is a dangerous individual who:
- Identified a soft target environment (Pride event, few adult
  men in the immediate area near the fish ladder)
- Approached and harassed a group of young women (minors)
- When confronted by an adult male in a non-threatening defensive
  posture (hands visible, verbal only), escalated to repeated
  physical assault
- Used a strike technique designed to cause serious injury
- After the initial confrontation ended, **RETURNED** to
  re-escalate (body check + additional strikes)
- Attempted to follow in the direction the girls had fled
- Presented a fabricated cover story to police

This is predatory, calculated behavior — not a momentary loss
of temper. He selected his targets, tested boundaries, escalated
when challenged, and attempted to re-engage after being
separated.

I am a straight white man. I was wearing rainbow suspenders at
Lansing Pride. He saw the suspenders and thought *fag*. He
thought that meant he could hit me.

He was wrong about what I am. And he was wrong about what he
could do. But he wasn't wrong that someone like him could walk
into a crowd of children at a Pride event and expect no
consequences — because that's the world we still live in until

My hands were visible the entire time. I never swung. I never
grabbed. I positioned myself between him and the girls until
they were gone, and then I documented everything.

I pressed charges. I have photos of him. I have a full prepared
statement. I will make every court date.
